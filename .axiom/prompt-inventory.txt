The CLI's `inventory` command (internal/builtin/inventory.go) currently has `fetch`, `list`, `validate` subcommands — but they're all STUBS that just print placeholder messages. The AC requires `inventory add --name <name> --url <url>` and `inventory list` to actually work with real inventory data.
